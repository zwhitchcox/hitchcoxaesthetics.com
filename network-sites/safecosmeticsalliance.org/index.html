How We Write These Guides

Every article is medically reviewed by Sarah Hitchcox, RN, BSN, a registered nurse who works with skin every day. We describe what the science and the regulations say, we name the limits of that evidence, and we do not claim an ingredient is dangerous without support. Nothing here is a diagnosis or a treatment plan. Read more about this site.
