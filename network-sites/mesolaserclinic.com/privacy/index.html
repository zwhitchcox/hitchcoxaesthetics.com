Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Overview

Meso Laser Clinic Guide (mesolaserclinic.com) is an educational website. We keep data collection to a minimum. This page explains what little we collect and why.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, fill out forms, or submit personal information. Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard server logs, which may include your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and the time of your visit. We use these logs only to keep the site running and secure.

Cookies and analytics

We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand which articles people read. We do not sell or share visitor data with advertisers, and we do not build profiles of individual visitors.

Third-party links

Articles on this site link to other websites, including Sarah Hitchcox Aesthetics, which maintains this site. Those websites have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to read them.

Fonts

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser requests those font files directly from Google, which may log that request under Google's own privacy policy.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Sarah Hitchcox Aesthetics through the contact information on hitchcoxaesthetics.com.
