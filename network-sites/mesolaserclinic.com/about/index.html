About This Site

What happened to Meso Laser Clinic?

This domain once belonged to Meso Laser Clinic, a laser and body-sculpting practice in the San Francisco Bay Area. That clinic closed years ago and is no longer in operation. This site is not that clinic, and it has no connection to its former owners or staff.

Who runs it now

The domain is now maintained by Sarah Hitchcox Aesthetics, a nurse-run med spa in Knoxville, Tennessee, with locations in Bearden and Farragut. Rather than let a long-standing laser-related domain go dark, we rebuilt it as a free educational resource about laser and light-based skin treatments.

What you will find here

Plain-language guides to common laser treatments: how laser hair removal works, what skin rejuvenation lasers actually do, how laser compares to IPL, safety for darker skin tones, and how to vet a laser clinic before you spend money there. Every article is medically reviewed by Sarah Hitchcox, RN, BSN, a registered nurse who performs these treatments in daily clinical practice.

What this site is not

This site does not sell treatments, book appointments, or provide medical advice. The content here is educational. Your skin, health history, and goals are unique, so always consult a qualified provider in person before starting any treatment.
