About This Site

A Site With History

Natural Skincare Recipes ran for nearly two decades, from 2005 to 2024, as an independent collection of at-home skincare recipes and guides. The original site retired, and its founders moved on. Rather than let the domain go dark, it found a new caretaker.

Who Maintains It Now

This site is now maintained by Sarah Hitchcox Aesthetics, a nurse-run medical spa in Knoxville, Tennessee, with locations in Bearden and Farragut. We keep it as an educational resource, and every article is medically reviewed by Sarah Hitchcox, RN, BSN.

All content here is new. We did not keep the previous site's articles. We write fresh recipes and guides, and we hold them to a simple standard: honest about benefits, explicit about risks, and clear about when a skin concern belongs with a professional.

What We Believe

  • Simple home recipes have a real place: gentle masks, scrubs, and rinses.
  • Patch test everything new. Skin is personal.
  • Some things do not belong in a kitchen: sunscreen and vitamin C serum top the list.
  • Sunscreen comes before every other skincare step.
  • Persistent skin problems deserve medical care, not more experiments.

A Note on Medical Advice

Articles on this site are educational. They are not a diagnosis or a treatment plan. If you have a skin condition, a reaction, or a spot that worries you, see a qualified clinician.
