Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Overview

Natural Skincare Recipes (naturalskincarerecipes.com) is a content site maintained by Sarah Hitchcox Aesthetics. We keep data collection minimal. The site has no accounts, no comment forms, and no newsletter.

Information We Collect

When you visit, our hosting provider records standard server logs. These can include your IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and referring site. We use this information to keep the site running, measure readership, and diagnose problems.

We do not ask for your name, email address, or health information on this site.

Cookies and Analytics

We may use basic analytics to understand which articles are read. Analytics data is aggregated and is not used to identify you. We do not sell or rent any visitor data.

External Links

Articles link to outside resources, including hitchcoxaesthetics.com, the site of the practice that maintains this resource. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

This site is written for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy over time. Changes appear on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through hitchcoxaesthetics.com.
