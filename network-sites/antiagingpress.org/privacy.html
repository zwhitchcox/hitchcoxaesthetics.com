Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Summary

This is a small static website with no accounts, no forms, no advertising, and no tracking cookies. We do not ask you for personal information, and we do not sell or share any.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information through this site. There is no contact form, no newsletter signup, and no comment system.

Like nearly every website, the server that delivers these pages records standard technical request information such as the address requested, the time, the browser type, and an IP address. This is used to keep the site running and secure. We do not use it to identify individual visitors.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies of its own, and it does not run advertising or analytics trackers.

Fonts and other third parties

Pages load a web font from Google Fonts. When your browser requests that font, Google receives the request and your IP address under its own privacy terms. If you prefer to avoid this, a browser extension that blocks third party font requests will stop it, and the site will still work.

Links to other sites

We link to other websites, including hitchcoxaesthetics.com. Once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of other websites.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Medical disclaimer

The articles here are general education. They are not medical advice, they do not create a clinician and patient relationship, and they are not a substitute for care from a qualified professional. Always seek the advice of your physician, dentist, or other qualified health provider with questions about a medical condition. Never disregard professional advice or delay seeking it because of something you read here. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change with it.

Contact

This site is maintained by Sarah Hitchcox Aesthetics in Knoxville, Tennessee. Contact details are on that website.
