## Automatic Sync

MapQuest does not expose a public API for editing its public business directory.

listings. MapQuest identifies Yext as its supported listings-management
partner. Continuous Google-to-MapQuest synchronization therefore requires a
paid listings-distribution product such as Yext, or a future private MapQuest
management API supplied by support.
