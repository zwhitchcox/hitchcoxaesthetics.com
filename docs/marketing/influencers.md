# Knoxville influencer program (gifted services)

Goal: local awareness and referral traffic from Knoxville creators, plus
real backlinks when the creator also runs a blog. Instagram bio links are
nofollow; the SEO value lives in BLOG posts, so bloggers-with-Instagrams
outrank Instagram-only accounts for our purposes.

Offer: a gifted service (facial, laser session, consult + treatment
credit). Rules:

1. Disclosure is required. The creator must mark posts #gifted or #ad
   (FTC). Never ask anyone to hide the arrangement.
2. Never condition the gift on POSITIVE coverage. Gift, and let them say
   what they think. (Same rule as press comps.)
3. If a blog link is part of the deal, that is a compensated link;
   do not dictate anchor text, and accept that honest creators may mark
   it rel=sponsored.
4. Weight-loss/GLP-1 content with creators needs care: no before/after
   promises, no medical claims in captions we co-author.
5. Track every gift and its retail value here; it is a marketing expense.
