### Safe Complimentary-Service Offer

The offer can be phrased as a no-strings media preview:

> We would be happy to host you for a complimentary service so you can evaluate
> the experience firsthand. There is no obligation to cover us or say anything
> positive, and any resulting coverage should disclose that the service was
> complimentary.

Do not ask for a "free service in exchange for a backlink." The FTC treats a
free service as a material connection that should be disclosed. Google asks
publishers to mark compensated placements with `rel="sponsored"` or
`rel="nofollow"`. The SEO goal should therefore be earned editorial coverage
based on a real story; paid or comped coverage should be evaluated primarily
for referral traffic and booked appointments.

Pretty Girl ranks first for lip filler and near the top for Botox and
microneedling despite a DR of only 1.4. Its useful backlinks include other
treatment providers citing its Botox timeline and laser aftercare articles.
This supports creating original, citation-worthy resources rather than buying
bulk links.

Recommended resources:

1. A first-time Botox timeline with clinician-reviewed expectations.
2. A Knoxville treatment price and appointment-duration report using aggregate
   internal data.
3. A laser hair reduction package guide explaining session count, spacing, and
   maintenance.
4. A GLP-1 consultation guide comparing what happens before, during, and after
   the first visit.
5. An anonymized annual Knoxville aesthetics trends report that local media can
   cite.

Each resource should have a clear expert author/reviewer, original data or
useful visuals, and a stable URL suitable for citations.

## Links Not to Copy

The audit found extensive automated link spam across the competitor set and
Sarah Hitchcox Aesthetics, including:

- Random `.shop` and `.store` domains promoting backlink services.
- PBN, "SEO anomaly," and guest-post marketplace pages.
- Unrelated foreign medical articles.
- Scraped directory pages with the wrong business attached.
- Fake testimonials claiming impossible ranking or traffic gains.
- Unrelated app, real-estate, and website-stat pages.

Do not buy or reproduce these links. They add noise, can complicate future
audits, and provide little local or topical authority.
