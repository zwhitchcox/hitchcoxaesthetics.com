By Sarah Hitchcox, RN · September 6, 2026

For adults receiving the labeled forehead-line regimen, the BOTOX Cosmetic
prescribing information specifies 20 units for forehead lines together with 20
units for glabellar lines, for a combined total of 40 units. The forehead-only
number and the combined treatment total describe different parts of the same
labeled regimen. [1]

## Understanding Botox Unit Counts in Knoxville

### Why do two quotes mention different totals?

Ask which areas each quote includes. “Forehead” may be used casually to describe
the horizontal forehead lines, the lines between the eyebrows, or both. A clear
estimate should identify the product and treatment areas before giving a total.
It should also explain whether consultation and follow-up are included in the
charge.

The glabella is the area between the eyebrows. The label pairs its treatment
with forehead-line treatment to reduce the potential for brow drooping. An
assessment of facial anatomy and movement matters; the online number is
background information, not a recommendation for your individual face. [1]

### Are Botox and Dysport units interchangeable?

No. The BOTOX Cosmetic label states that its potency units are specific to that
product and its testing method. They cannot simply be compared with, or
converted into, the units of other botulinum toxin products. A lower price per
unit therefore does not, by itself, establish which product or treatment plan
costs less overall. [1]

### What should I ask at a consultation?

Bring the questions behind your search. You may want to preserve a particular
expression, understand a previous treatment or compare two itemized estimates.
Ask the clinician to explain the proposed areas, expected limitations,
alternatives and follow-up plan in ordinary language. Share your medicine list
and relevant treatment history so the discussion is based on your circumstances.

BOTOX Cosmetic has risks, including a boxed warning about toxin effects
spreading beyond the treatment area. Seek immediate medical attention for
difficulty breathing, swallowing or speaking after treatment. Review the full
prescribing information and medication guide with a qualified clinician. [1]
