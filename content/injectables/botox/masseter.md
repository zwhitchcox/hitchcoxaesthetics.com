      - question: Is masseter Botox FDA-approved?
        answer: >-
          Botox is FDA-approved for forehead lines, frown lines, crow's feet

      - question: When will I see results?
        answer: >-
          Clenching and grinding often ease in one to two weeks. Slimming takes
          longer, because the muscle gets smaller slowly. Most people see a
          change at four to eight weeks and the full result at about three
          months.
      - question: How long does masseter Botox last?
        answer: >-
          Usually four to six months. When you repeat the treatment, the muscle
          can stay smaller for longer.
      - question: How much does masseter Botox cost?
        answer: >-
          Botox starts at $13 per unit. The masseter is a strong muscle, so it
          needs more units than an area like the forehead. Sarah tells you your
          units and your price at your free consultation.
      - question: Will it change how I chew or smile?
        answer: >-
          You can eat, talk and smile normally. For the first few weeks, some
          people feel their jaw get tired sooner when they chew tough food.
          Mild tenderness or a small bruise where the needle goes in can also
          occur.
      - question: Can Botox help TMJ pain?
        answer: >-
          Many people with jaw pain from clenching feel better when the muscle
          relaxes. But Botox does not cure TMJ disorders, and jaw pain has many
          causes. If you have jaw pain, see your dentist or doctor too.
