## A tinted mineral sunscreen for every day

sunbetter TONE SMART SPF 75 is a creamy lotion that protects with mineral
actives and leaves a hint of color. Most mineral sunscreens look a little white;
this one adapts to your skin tone, so you can wear it alone, as a primer under
makeup or in place of a light foundation.

## How to use it

Apply it generously 15 minutes before you go outside, and massage it in with
your fingertips. Reapply after 80 minutes of sweating or swimming, right after
towel drying, and at least every 2 hours. If you run or train outdoors, put it
on before you get dressed, and carry the
[sunbetter SHEER SPF 56 Mineral Sunscreen Stick](/skincare/sunbetter-sheer-spf-56-stick)
for touch-ups.
