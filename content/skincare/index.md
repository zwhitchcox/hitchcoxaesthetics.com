## Medical-grade skincare, matched to your skin

Good skin starts with what you do every day. At Sarah Hitchcox Aesthetics we
recommend medical-grade skincare, and the right products depend on your skin
type, your goals and how you spend your time outdoors. Instead of guessing from
a shelf, come in for a free skin consultation at our [Bearden](/bearden) or
[Farragut](/farragut) office.

## Sunscreen comes first

Every regimen we build starts with sunscreen. Years of sun show up as brown
spots, uneven texture and fine lines that arrive earlier than they had to, and
daily sunscreen is the simplest way to prevent them. We recommend a broad
spectrum mineral sunscreen with zinc oxide, SPF 30 or higher, and water
resistance for 80 minutes if you sweat or spend long stretches outside. Sarah
wears [sunbetter TONE SMART SPF 75](/skincare/sunbetter-tone-smart-spf-75)
and carries the
[sunbetter SHEER SPF 56 stick](/skincare/sunbetter-sheer-spf-56-stick) on long
runs.

If you run or train outdoors, put sunscreen on 15 minutes before you leave and
carry a mineral stick for your ears, nose, cheekbones, the back of your neck
and the tops of your shoulders. A hat with a brim and good sunglasses cut down
on how much you need to reapply.

## What to expect at your consultation

We look at your skin, ask about your current routine and what you would like to
change, and recommend a regimen that fits your skin and your schedule. If sun
damage is already showing, we will talk through treatments such as
[skin revitalization](/skin-revitalization),
[pigmented lesion reduction](/pigmented-lesion-reduction) and
[microneedling](/microneedling). You can pick up any products we recommend at
the office.
