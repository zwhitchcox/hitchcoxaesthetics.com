## A sunscreen stick for touch-ups

sunbetter SHEER SPF 56 is a solid mineral sunscreen in a stick. It goes on
sheer, leaves a smooth matte finish, and is small enough to carry anywhere,
which makes it easy to reapply when you are outside for hours.

## How to use it

Wipe off any sweat first, since a stick slides around on wet skin. Then swipe
it over your ears, nose, cheekbones, the back of your neck and the tops of your
shoulders. Reapply after 80 minutes of sweating or swimming, right after towel
drying, and at least every 2 hours. For your first coat of the day, we
recommend a lotion such as
[sunbetter TONE SMART SPF 75](/skincare/sunbetter-tone-smart-spf-75), then
the stick for touch-ups.
