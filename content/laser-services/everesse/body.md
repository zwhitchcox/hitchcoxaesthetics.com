## Body Skin Tightening in Knoxville

Loose skin on the abdomen is one of the most common concerns we hear about, and
one of the most stubborn. After significant weight loss, after pregnancy, or
simply with age, the skin loses the collagen and elastin that once kept it
snug against the body. No amount of core work rebuilds that structure, because
the issue is the skin envelope itself rather than the muscle underneath it.

Everesse addresses that directly. Focused radiofrequency energy heats the deeper
layers of skin while the surface stays actively cooled, which stimulates the
body to remodel collagen over the following weeks and months. The result is a
gradual firming and smoothing of the treated area, achieved without incisions,
anesthesia, or the recovery that a surgical tummy tuck requires.

Treatment sessions are comfortable and you return to normal activity the same
day. Most people notice the first changes within a few weeks, with continued
improvement as new collagen forms. Depending on how much laxity you are starting
with, we may recommend a single session or a short series.

Body tightening pairs naturally with our
[medical weight loss program](/weight-loss), since firming the skin after weight
loss is often the finishing step that makes the result feel complete. If you are
also considering facial treatments, see
[Everesse face tightening](/everesse/face) and
[Everesse neck tightening](/everesse/neck), or explore
[Everesse in Knoxville](/everesse) to build a plan across areas.
